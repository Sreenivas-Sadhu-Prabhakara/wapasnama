Money that quietly leaks

Every big khata app is sell-side. None track the purchase-side claims you raise against a distributor — short quantity, damaged goods, a wrong rate, a missing scheme discount. Those are near-weekly, and the credit note often lands short or never.

Wapasnama keeps the promised-vs-received tally so nothing slips.
